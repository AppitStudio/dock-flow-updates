VERSION: 1.97
DETAILS:

new: DockFlow in German, Japanese and Spanish – DockFlow follows your Mac's language, or the language you pick for DockFlow in System Settings → General → Language & Region → Applications. A few parts, such as the License screen and the color picker, are still in English for now.
new: Suggest a better translation – A new link in Settings → General opens the public DockFlow translation project, where anyone can suggest better wording.
new: Switch back when a Focus ends – DockFlow's Focus filter already applies a preset when a Focus turns on. Now it can also put your previous preset back when that Focus ends. It's off by default: turn on "Switch Back When Focus Ends" in Settings → Presets & Switching → Focus. If you pick another preset yourself during the Focus, DockFlow leaves it alone. Needs macOS 13 or later.
new: Open Focus Settings… – A new button in the same Focus section takes you straight to System Settings → Focus, where you choose the preset for each Focus.
new: Search in Settings – Type in the field at the top of the Settings sidebar to find any setting, by name or in plain words like "stop my dock jumping between screens". Pick a result and DockFlow opens the right page and highlights the setting.
new: AI assistants can undo their changes – When an AI app changes a DockFlow setting for you, it can now put the old value back. DockFlow won't undo a setting you have changed yourself since. AI apps can also turn the new Focus option on or off.
improved: Settings pages open instantly – Switching between pages no longer pauses, even the first time you open each one.
improved: Smoother settings changes – Toggles and sliders respond faster, and other pages stay up to date.
improved: Advanced page – The command line tool status shows without a pause.
bug fix: Talk Integrations no longer freezes DockFlow – Opening the page, or coming back to DockFlow while it shows, used to freeze the app for a moment. The cards now say "Checking…" briefly, then show their status.
