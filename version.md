new: Ready for more languages – DockFlow is now prepared to be translated into other languages. Everything still reads in English for now, and translations will follow in upcoming updates.

bug fix: AI approval wording – The AI assistant's approval sheet now reads correctly when an App Folder's name can't be shown.
